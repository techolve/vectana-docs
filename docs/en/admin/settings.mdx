The "System settings" tab under "Organization" holds settings for the whole Vectana instance. Only **group admins** see it.

## EOL warnings

![System settings tab](/screenshots/en/admin-system.png)

| Setting | Default | Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warn this many days before end of life | 90 days | 1–730 days |

Anything reaching end of life within this window becomes "Ending soon", is shown as such and is notified.

Saving re-checks EOL for every project right away. Anything that becomes "Ending soon" because the window grew is notified as usual.

## Vulnerability due dates (SLA) [#sla]

When a vulnerability has no due date of its own, it counts as **overdue** once it stays Open or Acknowledged for longer than these days after detection. The overdue numbers on the dashboard, the [organization dashboard](/getting-started/dashboard#org) and the weekly report use these days.

| Severity | Default |
| --- | --- |
| CRITICAL | 7 days |
| HIGH | 30 days |
| MEDIUM | 90 days |
| LOW | 180 days |
| Unknown | 90 days |

The range is 1–3650 days. A due date set on the vulnerability itself takes precedence ([assignee and due date](/guides/vulnerabilities#assignee-and-due-date)).

## Weekly report [#report]

When on, group admins, company admins and department heads get a weekly email summarizing their scope (open vulnerabilities, overdue items and EOL). It is on by default and sent on Mondays.

It goes out after the hour set on the server (9:00 Japan time by default). People who turned off email notifications don't get it.

## Other settings

The group name, check interval, email delivery and so on are server environment variables. See [Configuration](/self-hosting/configuration).
