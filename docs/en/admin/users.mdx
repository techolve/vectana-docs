## Roles and visibility [#roles]

A user can hold **any number** of the roles below. With none, they are a member who sees only the projects they're assigned to. Roles, and whether someone heads a department, decide which projects they see and what they can do.

| Who | Projects they see | Can |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Group admin | All | Manage every company, department and user, system settings, all customers and estimates |
| Company admin | All of their company's, deals included | Manage their company's departments and users, customers and estimates |
| Sales | All of their company's, deals included | Create and edit their company's customers and estimates, manage deals |
| Accounting | Their company's ordered projects (read only) | See their company's customers, estimates and unit costs |
| PMO | Ordered projects of every company (read only) | See all customers |
| Department head | Ordered projects of the departments they head and all sub-departments (plus assigned ones) | None |
| Member | Only projects they're assigned to | None |
| Partner (BP) user | Only projects they're assigned to | None |

- Deals (projects not yet ordered) are shown only to sales, administrators and the project's members ([Projects](/guides/projects#stages))
- Per-person unit costs (per day) are shown only to administrators, sales and accounting of the same company. They are used to cost estimates ([Sales: customers and estimates](/guides/sales#money))
- Projects you can't see don't appear in lists, and opening their URL directly shows "not found"
- Company admins, group admins and department heads can do everything an owner can in the projects they see
- In projects you're assigned to, your project role (owner, member, viewer) decides what you can do ([Projects](/guides/projects))

### Partner (BP) users

Users of a partner company have these limits:

- They can't have any role (not administrator, not sales)
- They don't see customers, stages or estimate amounts
- They can't create projects
- They can't be project owners
- They can't search for other users

## Invite a user

![Invitations tab](/screenshots/en/admin-invitations.png)

Invite people on the "Invitations" tab. Company admins invite to their own company; group admins to any company.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Email | The person's address |
| Display name | Optional; they can change it later |
| Roles | Optional; pick any number. Only group admins can grant group admin |
| Email language | The language of the invitation email and of the new account |
| Departments | Optional. Pick one or more (people can belong to several), one primary, and whether they head each one |

Press "Send invitation" to email them. Invitation links are valid for 7 days.

:::tip
If the email can't be sent, the invitation link is shown on screen. Copy it and pass it on directly, through chat for example.
:::

Pending invitations are listed and can be revoked. Inviting the same address again revokes the earlier invitation automatically.

## Edit a user

![Users tab](/screenshots/en/admin-users.png)

The "Users" tab lists users with their roles, departments, unit cost and status. Press "Edit" to change:

- Display name
- Roles (any number)
- Unit cost (per person-day), used to cost estimates
- Allow login (turn it off to stop the user from logging in)
- Departments (multiple, primary, head)

You can't change your own role or disable yourself. Company admins can't edit group admins or grant the group admin role.
