This page covers analytics on the Vectana documentation site (docs.vectana.techolve.org). It does not cover the Vectana app itself.

## Analytics

To improve the documentation, we use Google Analytics 4 to measure how pages are viewed. Information such as the pages you load, the referring site, your browser and device type, and your approximate region is sent to Google. We don't send personal information such as your name or email address.

For how Google handles this data, see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Consent

Google Analytics loads only if you choose "Accept" in the banner at the bottom of the page. If you decline or dismiss the banner, it doesn't load.

Your choice is stored in your browser. You can change it at any time from Cookie settings in the footer.

## Contact

For questions about this site, please open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/techolve/vectana-docs/issues).
