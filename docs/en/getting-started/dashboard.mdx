The dashboard summarizes **every project you can see**. Which projects those are depends on your [permissions](/admin/users).

![Dashboard](/screenshots/en/dashboard.png)

## Tasks

Open tasks come first, overdue and soonest due at the top. Switch between "Mine", "Team" and "Unassigned". See [Tasks](/guides/tasks#dashboard) for details.

## Summary cards

Six cards sit below the tasks.

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| Projects | Projects you can see |
| Packages | Packages registered across them |
| CRITICAL / HIGH | Open vulnerabilities with CRITICAL or HIGH severity |
| End of life | Packages and runtimes that are no longer supported |
| Ending soon | Those that will reach end of life soon (the window is set by an administrator) |

The CRITICAL, HIGH and EOL cards turn colored when they're not zero. Click a card to open the [vulnerability](/guides/vulnerabilities) or [EOL](/guides/eol) list.

Below them, three cards help you work through vulnerabilities.

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| Assigned to me (unresolved) | Open or acknowledged vulnerabilities assigned to you |
| Overdue | Unresolved vulnerabilities past their due date. Colored when not zero |
| Open and unassigned | Open vulnerabilities nobody is assigned to |

Click a card to open the vulnerability list with that filter. See [Vulnerabilities](/guides/vulnerabilities#details-panel) for setting assignees and due dates.

## Project status

For each project, the table shows open vulnerabilities by severity, EOL counts, the number of packages, and when it was last scanned.

Projects are ordered by CRITICAL count, then HIGH, then EOL — the ones at the top are the ones to look at first.

## Needs attention

At the bottom, switch between two tabs:

- **Vulnerabilities**: open vulnerabilities, highest severity first (up to 20). You can change their status right here
- **EOL**: packages and runtimes that have ended or are ending soon (up to 20)

To see everything, open "Vulnerabilities" or "EOL" in the sidebar.

## Organization dashboard [#org]

![Organization dashboard](/screenshots/en/org-dashboard.png)

"By organization" in the sidebar lists these numbers per company and department. A department's numbers include its sub-departments.

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Projects | Projects in that company or department |
| Open | Open vulnerabilities by severity |
| Overdue | Vulnerabilities still Open or Acknowledged past their due date, with the longest overdue days |
| EOL | Packages and runtimes that have ended or are ending soon |
| Nearest EOL | Days until the nearest EOL date (or days since it passed) |

The due date is the vulnerability's own, or else the [SLA in system settings](/admin/settings#sla). Use the arrows to expand or collapse departments, and the menu at the top right to pick a company. "Download CSV" saves the same table.

The tabs below list overdue vulnerabilities and upcoming EOL. Only projects you can see are counted, so a company or department you only partly see is marked "partial".

Admins and department heads receive the same summary by email as the [weekly report](/admin/settings#report).
