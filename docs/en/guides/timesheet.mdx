title: Timesheet and estimate vs actual
description: Enter hours week by week, paste from Excel, submit a week, and read a project's estimate vs actual.
---

"Timesheet" in the sidebar is where you enter the hours you worked per task, one week (Monday to Sunday) at a time. A project's "Estimate vs actual" tab compares those hours with its estimates.

## Enter hours [#enter]

1. "Add row" picks what you worked on
   - Your open tasks are listed; one click adds one
   - Or pick a project, then a task or "No task (project in general)"
   - For work outside projects, such as internal meetings or training, type a name
2. Type hours into each day's cell (decimals like 0.5 are fine)
3. "Save" stores the week

To continue last week's work, "Same rows as last week" copies the rows (without hours).

### Paste from Excel [#paste]

Copy a range in Excel or a spreadsheet, select the top-left cell to fill and paste: the hours fill several rows and days at once. Lines beyond the last row are skipped, so add rows first.

### The numbers [#numbers]

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Total | The row's hours this week |
| Left of estimate | The task's estimate (hours) minus everyone's hours on it so far. Negative means over the estimate |
| Day total | Hours that day. A day over 24 hours cannot be saved |
| Week / standard | The week's total against the standard 40 hours, and how much over |

## Submit a week [#submit]

When the week is complete, press "Submit week". Unsaved hours are saved first. You can still correct a submitted week, or "Withdraw" the submission.

## Whose timesheets you can see [#who]

| Who | Sees | Edits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Everyone | Their own | Their own |
| Department heads | People in their departments | No |
| Company administrators | Everyone in their company | Everyone in their company |
| Group administrators | Everyone | Everyone |

When you can see more than one person, switch between them at the top right.

## Estimate vs actual [#actuals]

A project's "Estimate vs actual" tab compares won estimates with the hours entered, at 8 hours per person-day. Partner (BP) users do not see it.

- **Effort (actual / estimate)**: the effort of won one-time estimates (initial and additional) against the hours so far. Amber above 90%, red above 100%
- **This month**: hours entered this month
- **Actual cost**: hours × each person's unit cost, shown only to those who see unit costs (sales, administrators, accounting; see [Who sees the money](/guides/sales#money))
- **By phase**: top-level tasks with sub-tasks count as phases. A phase's estimate is the sum of its tasks' estimates (hours). Hours not on any task go to "Outside phases"
- **By person**: hours per person

Build phases in the Gantt chart ([Tasks](/guides/tasks)) and give tasks an estimate in hours to see which phase is running over.
